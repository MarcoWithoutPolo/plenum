    How we work

    From farm to your cup in three steps

    1. We buy direct

      We visit our partner farms every harvest and pay well above fair-trade prices for the lots we love.

    2. We roast small

      Twelve-kilo batches on a restored 1962 drum roaster, profiled by hand for every coffee.

    3. We ship fast

      Orders leave the roastery the day they’re roasted, in compostable bags with a one-way valve.
